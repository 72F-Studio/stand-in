---
description: Interview evidence-backed stand-ins for your target users
---

Run a stand-in synthetic interview round. Load and follow the `stand-in`
skill (`skills/stand-in/SKILL.md` and its references).

It has two modes: Analysis (you run the whole study and report) and
Roleplay (the user asks the questions and you answer as the interviewee).
Use the one the user names, or ask.

Take the brief, gather web evidence before creating personas, build a panel
of three to six personas including a sceptic, and answer every interview
question with the in-character reply, its evidence and a confidence level.

Say "No data" when the evidence runs out, never invent sources, and tell the
user that the results are speculative.
